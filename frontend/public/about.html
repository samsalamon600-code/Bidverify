About the Platform

Public procurement on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) involves evaluating complex multi-document bids containing Technical Specifications, GST Certificates, PAN Cards, Udyam/MSME Registrations, Make in India (PPP-MII) Declarations, OEM Manufacturer Authorization Forms (MAF), and EPFO/ESIC Labour Statutory records.

Manual verification of dozens of bids per tender is time-consuming and prone to oversight. The AI-Powered Integrated Bid Compliance Verification Platform automates document ingestion, OCR text extraction, cross-document consistency verification, clause-by-clause requirement matching, and statistical anomaly detection while preserving 100% human oversight and page-level evidence traceability.

17-Point Verification Architecture
  • Multi-factor statistical risk & anomaly analysis
  • Udyam / MSME status and enterprise category verification
  • GSTIN format, status, and GSTR filing-status verification
  • Corporate PAN & MCA21 registration verification
  • Make in India (PPP-MII) local content threshold verification
  • EPFO & ESIC statutory establishment compliance checks
  • Startup India / NSIC / OEM Authorization (MAF) checks
  • DigiLocker issuer metadata verification adapter
  • Public debarment / watchlist screening adapter
  • Missing mandatory document & specification detection
  • Cross-document conflicting information detection (e.g., portal form vs PDF)
  • Configurable weighted compliance scoring & page-level evidence linking
